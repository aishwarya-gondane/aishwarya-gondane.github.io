About Me

Computational biologist with over 7 years of experience spanning industrial and academic research, currently focused on large-scale Perturb-seq and single-cell functional genomics in the context of sex chromosome biology. Experienced in deriving mechanistic biological insight from unbiased CRISPR screens and single-cell datasets — going beyond differential expression lists to identify the regulators and pathways driving specific cellular outcomes — using R, Python, and Shell scripting to build reproducible analysis pipelines.

Proven track record of managing multi-disciplinary projects, securing €85,000 in research grants, and communicating technical findings through high-impact publications and international presentations.
